**Client Management System**

**1. Core Features**

1.1 Chat Module

Details:

\- One chat group per project

\- Includes client and project team

1.2 Reports Module

Details:

\- Monthly reports uploaded by assigned user

\- Reports include:

\- Work progress

\- Images

Access:

\- View and download anytime

1.3 Invoices & Financial Summary

Details:

\- Total project value displayed

\- Auto-update after VO approval

Payment Status:

\- Pending

\- Paid

\- Partially Paid

Updates:

\- Managed by Accounts team

1.4 Variation Orders (VO)

Details:

\- Created by Technical Office

\- Includes:

\- Title

\- Description

\- Cost

\- Attachments

Client Action:

\- Approve or Reject

1.5 Additional Sections

\- Tender Documents: Uploaded once

\- Schedules: Updated regularly

\- Drawings & Renders: 2D & 3D uploads

**2. Implementation Considerations**

2.1 User Roles & Permissions

\- Planning → Reports

\- Technical → VO & Drawings

\- Accounts → Financial updates

2.2 Partial Payments

\- Use simple status updates

\- Avoid complex accounting systems

2.3 Admin Interface

\- Manage uploaded content

\- Assign responsibilities

\- Control client visibility

**3. User Account & Access Management**

\- Admin creates username and password for each client

\- Client logs in to access their project portal

\- Client can change their password later
